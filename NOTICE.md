# Sources and licensing notice

The current teaching, legal reasoning, and transcript editing references are original syntheses of selected passages in 15 works. Five books inform the teaching reference. Six books and Hohfeld’s 1913 article inform legal reasoning. Two copyediting books and the guide to the Black Women Oral History Project transcripts inform editorial practice. The [primary reading record](fidelity-ledger/original-sources.json) identifies the editions and inspected passages. The runtime references give authors and locators for the relevant material.
