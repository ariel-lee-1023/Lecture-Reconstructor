### Comparison with the specialist reference selections

The teaching project's multimedia handbook reference identifies chapter 44, source conflict, and the limits of the chapter’s coverage. The new teaching module takes the original chapter's Paris-opera comparison down to the critic, the lighting technician, the changed condition, and what the short-delay recall result can support. The student gets to inspect why reconciling the two accounts as a mixed reception would dispose of the very question the comparison raises.

The prior legal materials already contain an *Able* analysis and a procedure for extracting precedents. An independent example added here follows the separate vote groupings in *Dodd* into the conflicting readings in *Harper*. One majority can support the order while a different grouping agrees on a legal question. A reader who assumes that each vote for the order endorsed one common ground would misteach what the subsequent court was able to apply. The example and its limits are traced to Duxbury’s original discussion.

The copyediting selection in the transcript project covers percentage points, relative change, quoted speech and a serial-comma example about departments. The final editorial module adds an error corrected inside an utterance, a quoted amount whose scope changes when words are cut, and an exhibit whose connecting line suggests unobserved ranks. They change distinct decisions about preserving a correction, restoring the missing condition and reading what a chart measures. The sources for those additions are the cited pages of the original handbook. The archive reference previously distinguished recordings, transcripts and catalogue access in general. The new module works through the incomplete Hedgeman entry and its separate permissions and identifiers.

## Direct statements, attribution and project decisions

The semantic review examined each module’s worked passage, consequential premises in its framework and decision sections, and their locators. In the teaching module, the decisive new evidence is Jenny’s later response with a different set. The final copyedit puts the interpretation next to the observation and retains the uncertainty about timing and causation. Attribution to Neuman and the book remains where it identifies who observed the case. The opera passage retains the two speakers because interchanging their observations would change the comparison.

In the legal module, the reader can reconstruct the two vote groupings from the table, then follow their consequences in *Harper*. Judicial names and the scope of the order carry the reasoning. The name of an author alone could not supply that reasoning. In the *Able* passage the defense account remains attributed as a theory. Treating a signed blank check as proof of what the taxpayer believed would erase a live premise. Both sections retain printed source locators. Current precedential force would require checking the competent jurisdiction and the judgments themselves.

In the editorial module, the example on p. 213 of *The Copyeditor’s Handbook* supplies an explicit correction from product line 405 to 4055. The final text attaches the prediction to the corrected product and preserves its prospective force. Section 3 distinguishes dots already in a source from an omission by an editor or translator. Section 7 dates historical access terms to compilation and preserves the brackets’ editorial function. The final copyedits to these sections and to the Jenny passage replace author-reporting frames with the supported relationship or condition. The original-source register keeps the digests from the passage reviews alongside those for the final wording.

**Project applications.** The core asks a lecturer reconstructing a weak class to recover the strongest plausible route to a mistaken answer, confront it with the operative material, and preserve any narrower conclusion that survives. The source-led notice lesson in the workflow is a fictional project example. Neither its dispatch rule nor its claim deadline comes from the legal publications. Similarly, comparing a student’s objection with a memo’s evidence is a project use of teaching and editorial distinctions. The books do not prescribe the run schema, stable anchors, recovery sequence, evaluation fixtures or completion gate. Recognition-specific repairs use the project’s workflow. The editorial sources support decisions about wording, numbers, and documentary identity without establishing the sounds on a missing recording.
