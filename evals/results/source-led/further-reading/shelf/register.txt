South Bank Study Museum. May 1912 receiving register. Column headings: identifier | day received for examination | object | source of delivery. Entry A-37 | 14 May | River at Dawn, oil on panel | North Quay Carriers. Reverse of A-series catalogue card: ‘A’ numbers follow the receiving register. A receipt records entry into the examination room. Lending and purchase decisions are kept separately. This register does not identify the owner.
